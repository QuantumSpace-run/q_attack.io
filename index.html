🧠 the convergence lesson

The adversary's stack acts as one system:

💻 A quantum computer broke your legacy crypto (harvest-now-decrypt-later) and simultaneously optimizes the drone swarm against your radar coverage.
🧲 Quantum sensing found your silent submarine.
📡 Quantum communication (QKD) secures the swarm's control links — which is why no electronic warfare could read them.


The winning counter was likewise convergent: your quantum sensing sweep didn't just save the submarine — it physically located the QKD relays feeding the swarm, while radar fusion starved the optimizer of a predictable target.


Key takeaway for decision-makers: Post-quantum migration takes years and must begin before the emergency — the decision to commit takes 90 seconds. Diagnose before you act.
